# Project proposal

**Project Title:** University Information Assistant for Students  
**Project Name:** IlliniInfo  
**Team Members:** Nick Chaudhari, Kayetan Jarzabek, Yoyo Lin  
**Project Coordinator:** Nick Chaudhari

## Project Description

IlliniInfo will help UIUC students find academic information through a search interface returning relevant passages and official source links. Our initial focus is undergraduate Computer Science degree requirements and related course information.

Students often search several university pages to understand requirements. We will bring relevant degree and policy passages together while preserving headings, source links, and catalog-year context.

For a manageable prototype, we will select a small collection of public Academic Catalog pages and relevant university policy sections. We will collect the text in a shared dataset, divide the text into searchable passages, and compare TF-IDF and BM25 ranking. A Streamlit interface will accept student questions and display ranked passages with citations.

We will evaluate approximately 25-30 representative questions using manual relevance judgments, Precision@5, and mean reciprocal rank. We will also check whether displayed passages support the requested information.

Broader degree coverage, Course Explorer integration, and generated summaries are optional extensions after the core search workflow functions.

Tentatively, Nick will lead source collection and organization. Kayetan will lead preprocessing, indexing, and retrieval. Yoyo will lead interface development and integration. All members will contribute to evaluation, testing, documentation, and presentation work.
