## Project Description

IlliniInfo will help UIUC students find academic information through a search interface that returns relevant passages and links to official sources. Our initial focus will be undergraduate Computer Science degree requirements and related course information. Students often navigate multiple university pages to understand requirements; our tool will bring supporting information together while preserving its source and context. Compared with browsing individual university pages, IlliniInfo will bring relevant degree and policy passages together in one search interface, with citations and catalog-year context. For a manageable prototype, we will curate a small collection of public Academic Catalog pages and selected university policy sections relevant to these questions. We will clean and divide documents into searchable passages, retain headings and catalog-year information, and compare TF-IDF and BM25 ranking. A Streamlit interface will accept student questions and display ranked passages with citations. This approach emphasizes verifiable evidence rather than unsupported answers. We will evaluate approximately 25-30 representative questions using manually judged relevance, Precision@5, and mean reciprocal rank. We will also check whether displayed passages support the requested information. Broader degree coverage, Course Explorer integration, and generated summaries are optional extensions after the core search workflow functions. Tentatively, Nick will lead collection and preprocessing, Kayetan will implement indexing and retrieval, and Yoyo will lead interface development. All members will contribute to evaluation, testing, and documentation.
